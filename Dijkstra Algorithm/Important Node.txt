We have two methods to write Dijkstra Algorithm

Method 1 will give T.C. of O(V^2) and S.C. of O(V)

Method 2 will give T.C. of O(V log E) and S.C. of O(V + E)

We can see that Method 2 is better than Method 1.. But it is not true for all the cases.

If we have a Dense Graph (edges connected with nodes that are close to complete graph), i.e. No. of edges is approx. equal to V^2

The Updated T.C. of Method 1 and Method 2 will be O(V^2) and O(V^2 log V).
We can see that Method 1 is performing better than Method 2.

If we have a Sparse Garph (edges connected to nodes that are very minimum), i.e. No. of edges is approx. equal to V

The Updated T.C. of Method 1 and Method 2 will be O(V^2) and O(E log V).
We can see that Method 2 is performing better than Method 1.

So, Remeber this before applying, If the graph is Dense than Method 1, and if The graph is Sparse than Method 2
